# Customer outcomes and research

## Purpose trigger and inputs

Use WF06 before a material experience decision and after change, complaints or new barriers. The research lead owns method validity; the privacy owner establishes data permissions and safeguards; the service owner funds action. Inputs are selected outcomes, tasks, access contexts, release, protocol, instrument/data dictionary and decision purpose. A survey is one evidence source, not a legal conformity test.

The IDSO dataset is the organisational outcome model. Preserve its IDs: IDSO-01 perception, 02 meaning, 03 operation, 04 navigation, 05 understanding, 06 technology/settings, 07 safety, 08 support, 09 accurate completion, 10 effort/time, 11 recovery, 12 autonomy, 13 privacy/security/consent, 14 equivalent service, 15 continuity, 16 confidence/control, 17 dignity, 18 satisfaction, 19 collaboration and 20 enjoyment where material. Select relevant outcomes with rationale; do not rename them as statutory EAA clauses.

## Functional lenses and contextual needs

Annex I VII provides these a–k criteria: use without vision; limited vision; without colour perception; without hearing; limited hearing; without vocal capability; limited manipulation/strength; limited reach; minimising photosensitive seizure risk; limited cognition; and privacy. Their legal applicability follows [G01](01-scope-and-applicability.md). Broader use to plan inclusive research is recommended practice.

Ask voluntary, task-relevant needs questions rather than requiring diagnosis. Allow multiple selections, no particular need reported, prefer not to answer and missing responses. Privacy is evaluated across accessible modes, not as a respondent category. Consider intersections, fatigue, pain, fluctuating capacity, environment, language, situational constraints and chosen technology without claiming these are additional statutory categories. Do not infer disability from keyboard, caption or screen-reader use. Design notifications with appropriate equivalent cues and user control; simultaneous visual, audible and haptic output is not universally suitable.

## Collect complementary evidence

Observe realistic complete tasks, including confirmation of the correct end state, assistance, recovery and abandonment. Ask about comprehension, confidence, effort, dignity and satisfaction. Distinguish chosen support from forced dependence. Include people unable to enter or finish the service through accessible recruitment, support and alternative feedback routes. End-of-journey respondents alone cannot represent excluded customers.

Use accessible participation and questions, familiar technology where appropriate, informed participation and clear withdrawal limits. Minimise collection before it happens. Approved recording for research differs from covert extraction. Third-party collection and aggregation do not automatically make data anonymous or lawful; pseudonymised data can remain identifiable. Review provider roles, access, locations, retention, deletion, training reuse and small-cell disclosure before collection. Participation consent and the processing legal basis are separate decisions.

Validate the actual survey instrument, scale endpoints, missing-data codes, export schema, period and provider operations. This package assumes no functioning ASK API or validated universal questionnaire. Quarantine incompatible exports. Keep individual evidence in controlled systems; publish only suitably protected insight and methods.

## Measures and interpretation

| Measure | Operational definition to set before collection |
|---|---|
| Accurate completion | Verified correct outcomes divided by defined task attempts; report independent and assisted completion separately. |
| Effort/time | Duration distribution and observed/reported demand for the same task, including support queues and recovery. |
| Satisfaction | Defined item, scale, valid response count, distribution and context; not a proxy for successful independent use. |
| Equivalence | Compare material availability, quality, timing, cost, privacy and recourse; identical interfaces are unnecessary. |

For every measure record unit/scale, denominator, collection method, context, instrument version, uncertainty and limits. Overlapping groups cannot be summed into unique participants. Compare appropriate skills, learning, tasks and environments; sighted mouse use is not a universal ideal. The old 20/50/100% timing bands are illustrations, not acceptance thresholds. Define local thresholds with rationale and authority.

Statistical significance needs an appropriate question, design and available data. Means and counts do not invent variance. Before/after change alone does not establish cause. Small qualitative studies can reveal severe reproducible exclusion without estimating prevalence. Report missing people, non-response, negative cases and practical importance alongside averages. Do not expose participants to unsafe flashing merely to collect user evidence.

## Findings outputs and next workflow

Use `supported`, `partly supported`, `not supported`, `insufficient evidence`, `not evaluated` or `not relevant with rationale` per selected outcome. Preserve task/context/version, observations, uncertainty and contradictory evidence. A favourable satisfaction finding cannot erase an applicable failure; a technical pass cannot establish a good experience. Route material barriers to WF09 and findings to WF07. [Evidence review](05-evidence-conformity-and-claims.md) assesses any legal implication; there is no automatic outcome-to-conformity conversion.
