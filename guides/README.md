# Guide retrieval

Read the root [AGENTS contract](../AGENTS.md), then the [document index](../control/document-index.yaml). The six guides are proposed practitioner guidance; they are not approved product conclusions. Each guide's front matter names sources, prerequisites and outputs.

| Decision | First section | Required context |
|---|---|---|
| Is this subject covered? | [G01 Article 2 routing](01-scope-and-applicability.md#article-2-routing) | Operator, consumer service/product, jurisdiction, dates |
| Which methods and criteria? | [G02 Correct the mapping approach](02-requirements-and-assessment.md#correct-the-mapping-approach) | Approved scope, capability inventory, standard version |
| Who delivers and accepts? | [G03 Human responsibilities](03-delivery-and-procurement.md#human-responsibilities) | Funded owners and delegation |
| What do customers experience? | [G04 Measures and interpretation](04-customer-outcomes-and-research.md#measures-and-interpretation) | Protocol, data permissions, outcome definitions |
| What can evidence support? | [G05 Reach a bounded requirement conclusion](05-evidence-conformity-and-claims.md#reach-a-bounded-requirement-conclusion) | Current requirement/evidence set |
| What changes after release? | [G06 Correct causes and verify outcomes](06-monitoring-and-corrective-action.md#correct-causes-and-verify-outcomes) | Event, affected decisions, action authority |

Keep linked limits and decision conditions with retrieved chunks. Do not load every specialist source for an ordinary task. Load the relevant module when the capability inventory warrants it, and retain unresolved assessment methods as gaps.
